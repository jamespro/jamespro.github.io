Privacy Policy — Personal Assistant

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Scope

This application is private, single-user software operated by its owner. It serves only the owner's own Google account and does not collect data from, or provide services to, any other users.

Google user data accessed, and why

  • Gmail (read, send, modify) — to summarize messages for the owner, track bills and commitments, and send mail only when the owner directs.
  • Calendar — to read events and maintain the owner's reminders.
  • Drive, Docs, Sheets — to read, organize, and edit the owner's own files at the owner's request.
  • Contacts (read-only) — to recognize people the owner refers to.

Data handling

Google data is processed only on the owner's own computer by the owner's own software. Access credentials are stored only on that computer. The application operates no public server, runs no analytics, and sells or shares nothing. Data is not retained on any external system owned by this application.

Revocation and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google account permissions page; local credential files can then be deleted from the owner's machine.
