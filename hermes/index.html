Hermes

Personal AI assistant — single-user, privately operated.

This page identifies the OAuth application used by Hermes, a personal AI assistant operated by its owner for private, non-commercial use on personal devices.

Hermes integrates with the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Contacts) to provide personal automation — morning briefings, bill tracking, reminders, and document drafting. The application is configured for one user only: the account owner.
