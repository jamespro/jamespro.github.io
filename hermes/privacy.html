Privacy Policy — Hermes

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Who this applies to

Hermes is a single-user personal AI assistant. This policy covers its OAuth access to one Google account: the owner's own account (jwprickitt@gmail.com). Hermes does not serve, collect from, or store data of any other users.

What Google data is accessed and why

  • Gmail (read, send, modify) — to surface bills and commitments in daily briefings, and to send messages only when the owner explicitly directs.
  • Calendar — to read events and maintain the owner's reminder workflow.
  • Drive, Docs, Sheets — to read and organize the owner's own files and documents at the owner's request.
  • Contacts (read-only) — to recognize people mentioned in the owner's messages and notes.

Where data lives

Google data is processed on the owner's personal computer by locally-run software. Access credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored only on that machine. The assistant does not operate a public server, does not run analytics, and does not sell or share data with third parties. Standard AI-provider processing may occur when the owner explicitly asks the assistant to perform a task involving that data, consistent with the assistant's own terms.

Retention & deletion

No Google data is retained on any external server owned by this application. Removing the application's access in your Google account permissions page revokes all access immediately; local token files can be deleted from the owner's machine.
